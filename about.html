Who We Are

Machinery for paper, printing and packaging businesses

Prima Machinery Co. is a manufacturer and supplier of paper, printing and packaging machinery based in Amritsar, Punjab, India.

We work with printing presses, carton makers, corrugated box manufacturers, paper converters and machinery dealers. Our range includes paper cutting machines, paper corrugation machines and the PRIMA-35 die punching machine, along with other machines used in paper and packaging production.

Every enquiry is handled directly by our team, led by Vikas Gupta, so customers get clear answers about which machine suits their material, sheet size and production requirement.
